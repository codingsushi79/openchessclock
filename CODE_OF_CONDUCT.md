# Code of Conduct

OpenChessClock follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## The short version
- Be kind and patient. People here range from first-time solderers to professional engineers.
- Critique designs and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language, in issues, pull requests, discussions or
  anywhere else the project lives.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming someone meant harm.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow this code.
To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/codingsushi79) instead of posting in public.
